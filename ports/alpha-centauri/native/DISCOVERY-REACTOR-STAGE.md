technology-discovery caller stage. It is not an ownership grant, complete award,
research/turn action, active authority, UI callback or persistence commit.

publishing any partial result. Such a path needs a full current decoded working

or repeatedly querying the initial World is not a valid substitute.

For single early results use `has_existing_return()`/`existing_return()` and

No fake UID is substituted and caller follow-up is not skipped. Live retirement,
SMAX naming provenance, AI/network/modal, auto-design and further discovery effects
remain held. Zero pairs do not trigger the reactor popup even for the local owner.

All failures preserve the complete previous plan and all inputs/World records.
World/raw/decoded, rules, facts, labels, nested owned base arrays and repeated-prune
input/output aliases reject. Private owning-plan swaps grant no World capability.
