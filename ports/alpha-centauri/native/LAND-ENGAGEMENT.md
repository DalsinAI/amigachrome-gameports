# Owned conventional land engagement staging

`stage_land_engagement` joins the existing original-backed strength, health,
CRT draw, retreat initialization, destination and stack-storage stages in a
private owning plan. It uses CURRENT decoded records, current monument/history
bytes and the complete opaque wire baseline. It does not reload the old save
and discard later owned changes. Every borrowed raw-view span is rebound to the
plan's storage; history has separate ownership. Inputs and an earlier successful
output remain unchanged on failure.

The domain is ordinary nonmissile conventional LAND versus LAND, fight flags0,
attacker plans0..3 and defender plans0..3 or artifact12, real reactor profiles1..4.
The supplied observation carries the current CRT state, incoming direction,
network fact and pod coefficients. These are explicit numeric/context facts,
not a claim of active original TLS, UI, session or legal-attack authority.
The World's movement RNG is preserved. Combat uses its separate214013 CRT
transition; it is not seeded from the map, footer or movement stream.

Each round applies the source's draw order, modulo rolls, defender-wins-ties
rule and one-byte conventional damage. On a surviving defender hit, the retreat
counter decreases by one. Its first exhaustion requests the actual current
neighbor/pod/fungus/treaty selection. No destination disables further attempts;
a selected destination stages the real final solitary stack relocation. Capacity,
raw Carrier, and SMAX design-owner FREEABIL-grant retreat profiles are held
conservatively where the complete transport behavior is not qualified.

The result stops at an attacker/defender casualty boundary or immediately after
retreat storage. A casualty tag means **pending casualty effects**, not deletion,
a kill award or completion. A retreated frame still does not acknowledge later
presentation/events. It does not expose player input or commit the caller World,
either random stream, or a session. No general battle/turn/AI completion claim.
The separate SMAX naval disengagement counter requires SEA actors and is outside
this LAND domain; its chance branch is not fabricated for land combat.

Real reactors1..4 bound each actor's initial health by40. Every accepted ordinary
round damages exactly one living actor by one, so the80-round work bound cannot
truncate a supported fight. Wider raw reactors remain a separate single-round
leaf domain; their byte wrap could prevent finite engagement termination and
is not silently treated as a normal finite battle here.

## Validation and limits

The final26 synthetic Worlds cover4,212 attempted plans. Host ASan/UBSan and
actual independent Linux68040 execution produce byte-identical summaries and
EVERY current vehicle/tile wire byte for2,352 accepted plans and1,860 rejections.
There are22,559 rounds/39,730 actual owned CRT draws,28 staged retreats,1,339
attacker casualty boundaries and985 defender casualty boundaries. Complete
unrelated decoded records, preferences, map metadata, history, monuments, opaque
baseline and caller movement RNG remain unchanged. Failed result replacement,
low memory, input/output aliases, current-record/history/monument changes and
input destruction are checked. The dedicated retreat fixtures use real solitary
stacks; earlier duplicated-coordinate fixtures correctly exercised casualties
but could not exercise successful retreats.

The independent Python interpretation composes the existing source-derived
numeric models, then compares all output vehicle/tile bytes. Additional private
original executions prove the unchanged post-defender-hit counter gate in4,032
contexts EACH edition: zero/dead short circuits, real health arithmetic and
signed quantum countdown. These fragments stop at the next-round or destination
boundary; each has two explicit observation jumps and no helper patch/callback.
Existing strength, CRT arithmetic, decision, initialization, destination and
complete stack-helper proofs retain their own documented scopes.

This is NOT execution of the whole original battle routine. The original Windows
TLS getter, full CRT/session initialization, rendering and casualty/event tails
remain unexecuted. The composed owned plan is checked against the independent
interpretation and individually proved source stages; no fake original callback
or full-loop differential claim is made.

Two final normal Amiga SDK builds are byte-identical. Actual AmigaOS execution,
remote final-head CI, frontend controls, owning legal attack/commit, combat CRT
persistence, remaining casualty/events, transport retreat, broader psi/air/sea,
AI/diplomacy and turns remain unqualified. The watch preview is untouched.
