# Owned conventional LAND pair resolution

`stage_land_resolution` composes the frozen numeric engagement, ordinary casualty
and LAND artifact-capture stages into one owned current World result. Its tagged
summary distinguishes attacker deletion, defender deletion, defender capture0 and
retreat. A deletion reports source casualty return1 and dense attacker/defender
indices after compaction; capture reports0 and preserves indices/count. Retreat
reports no casualty call. Combat CRT state is copied explicitly into the summary,
separate from the unchanged World movement RNG and unchanged caller facts.

Both actors must be nonnative, solitary and noncargo. Raw Carrier, nonzero cargo
capacity and expansion design-owner FREEABIL grants are conservatively held.
The existing conventional nonmissile LAND numeric/profile/reactor restrictions
remain. Multi-unit stack and full cargo cleanup are not silently approximated by
one deletion. Both-zero health is held. Multiplayer/logging/visible BaseWin and
missing observations are held/rejected; saved metadata does not establish offline
runtime or local authority.

The caller routes are asymmetric. Source SMAC51a160/SMAX506130 stack wrappers
receive counterpart−1 on attacker loss. Defender loss passes the attacker to the
casualty routine; artifact capture can therefore preempt its interlude gate.
Raw argument6 is supplied separately for the two branches. It is not inferred
from a generic winner or forced to the attacking faction. Inspected SMAX caller
506af0 uses original defender faction on the attacker-loss stack call, and
original attacker faction on its direct defender-loss call. Base51ab20 contains
reused decompiler variables; full instruction lineage remains unexecuted. Earlier
notes about the direct defender-loss call do not qualify every caller branch.

This is an immediate numeric/body-state composition, not the full original
battle caller. COMBATWIN popup handling, opponent/faction statistics, diplomacy,
other cargo/stack cases, later draws and dialogs remain mandatory separate work.
No dialog outcome, acknowledgment or legal attack/session authority is fabricated.
Interlude-required ordinary defender casualties stay held transactionally.

The output owns only its final current frame. A casualty first uses the staged
engagement frame, so combat damage and retreat/RNG summary cannot be lost by
cloning the imported baseline. Artifact capture recalculates speed after ownership
transfer. An intermediate clone is released only after the final result succeeds.
The candidate clone budget accounts for both intermediate/final heaps; existing
caller output and transient helper scratch allocations are outside that budget,
following parent-stage conventions. A failed stage leaves the previous complete
output intact. All raw imported bytes/views remain baseline, not a current save.

Retreat exposes its owned frame and numeric summary but deliberately returns no
RemovalRuntime. The existing engagement stage has not established every original
retreat-runtime side effect. Absence is explicit; it must not be treated as an
unchanged or zero initialized live runtime. Casualty/capture results expose their
qualified component runtime. No result is committed to a session or player UI.
