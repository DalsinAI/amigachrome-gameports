# Fresh Human Worker Resolution

`world_worker_resolution` closes the earlier candidate's worker/specialist and
psych prerequisites for the bounded directed-human stage1 profile. It derives
its candidate internally and accepts no caller-provided resource or drone
summary. Commerce and alien-grid income remain explicit authoritative
current-stage facts; commerce generation and a global trade/worker fixed point
are separate stages. There is no End Turn action or world mutation here.

The policy is pinned Thinker default `mod_base_yield`,
`dabe62f2d1abba03de40f3dd3048494843f6a9af`, MIT. Original full worker allocation
is unavailable. Active citizen governors, AI, editor and SMAX reject. The
implemented directed branch derives the actual specialist eligibility and
weights, reconsiders the greedy prefix at the specialist-substitution boundary,
and retains valid stored unused specialist slots. Empty original worker masks
manage all specialist types; overassignment alone resets workers while
preserving eligible stored types. Invalid nibble IDs are safely repaired rather
than indexed outside the citizen table. Above16 specialist behavior belongs to
the same pinned allocation policy. A separate source review checked these
details, including preserving unused eligible Doctor/Technician slots.

Every trial computes current selected-tile income, nutrient use, support and
mineral multipliers/ecology, HQ inefficiency, economy/labs/psych allocation and
all five psych/drone stages. Newly assigned workers may be removed in reverse
choice order until riots are prevented, no new choices remain, or the pinned
raw-tile-mineral protection predicate stops further removal. Preserved manual
worker assignments are not silently discarded. A fresh `requires_riot_event`
can remain true: that is an explicit result requiring the real event stage,
not a successful no-riot shortcut. Starvation is also real output, not hidden
by rearranging balances or fabricating income.

The final nutrient aggregate and nine hypothetical efficiency totals use
actual stage1 against the unchanged original current faction aggregates.
Intermediate trial evaluations never accumulate those totals repeatedly.
The resulting current assignment, specialist nibbles, full fresh intake,
mineral facts/results, food, pre-allocation energy, allocated income and fresh
psych are returned atomically for the owning scheduler to consume.

`world_mineral_accounting` is the narrow fresh-material binder shared by the
old inspection wrapper and this new stage. The old wrapper still computes its
own qualified inspection intake; no old worker guard was removed. The new
binder derives all physical project/facility/faction/map facts and calls the
existing recovered mineral leaf. Its transient inputs must come from the
current owning worker/convoy/support/ecology stage; the binder itself does not
grant those inputs authority.

`world_terraform_ecology_for_workers` recomputes improvement damage for the
proposed mask, including after every trim. This fixes the candidate's earlier
old-mask ecology seam. The original ecology query delegates with its original
mask semantics; 527 existing independent-world regressions remain byte-exact.
A new controlled candidate starts from a centre-only mask on improved terrain
and proves that the proposed mask's raw ecology differs from the old mask.
That controlled change also passes genuine native execution, guest RC0, all
838 output bytes identical to its independent oracle (SHA-256
`247029f0140a49ec2ad6e82dfe5f46b6ddf2f6cb8de34d2d4ee3b71bc1711fbd`).

Validation: strict C++ builds and ASan/UBSan pass 27 independent wire/component
world cases, with 22 accepted and seven actual worker trims. Cases include
preserved directed assignments, current physical modifiers, eligible unused
specialist slots, above16 specialists, empty/overassigned masks, cured Doctor
riots, mineral-protected unresolved Technician riots, stage/editor/SMAX guards
and late energy overflow. The complete host aggregate SHA-256 is
`f5fe224ca414fe00f4f19a2f001644c0adff1e70bfe0cd0b743891d0db67f718`.
The mineral binder preserves all 141 prior imported-world cases with output
SHA-256 `ac131e5c48f487e3fc4bde63ed804d9053f1b048578fd902ad6bc726cf75325d`.
Native execution is recorded separately after genuine guest runs; a HUNK build
alone is not counted as native runtime proof.

Use `test_world_worker_resolution.py` and
`build_world_worker_resolution_probe.sh`. The public result header is
`world_worker_resolution.h`; it does not expose a mutable world or a reusable
turn-authority token.
