1. Your game files

This page contains no Firaxis data. Choose your own installed Sid Meier's Alpha Centauri (and optionally Alien Crossfire) folder, or pick the files. They are read into this tab's memory only; nothing is uploaded.

The title screen needs openinga.pcx, ALPHC___.TTF and arialn.ttf (Arial Narrow, from your system fonts if your install does not have it). New games need alpha.txt (or alphax.txt for Crossfire) and the faction files; the map needs texture.pcx, Units.pcx, console.pcx, faction art and Arial.ttf or arialn.ttf. Movies (.wve) are skipped. Names match regardless of case.
